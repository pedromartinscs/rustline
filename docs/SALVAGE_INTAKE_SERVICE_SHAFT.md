# Salvage Intake — East Service Shaft

The east service shaft is a canonical part of Salvage Intake. The base shaft is generated by `Assets/Editor/RustlineSalvageIntakeSetup.cs`; current production-route refinements are then applied deterministically by `RustlineSalvageIntakeRouteRefinementSetup` through the single **Tools -> Rustline -> Apply Production Setup** command.

The temporary scene-specific service-shaft overlay tool has been retired. The authoring workflow should use the production setup rather than individual legacy menu commands.

## Geometry contract

All geometry remains on the canonical `16 px = 1 u` grid. Hidden collision stays simple and authoritative; production skins may diverge visually while preserving the gameplay envelope.

Current production service-shaft geometry:

- safety floor extends beneath the entry/shaft through `x=33`, `y=-4..-1`;
- lower shaft entry: `x=26..27`, `y=0..3` remains completely open, giving **4 u / 64 px** of standing clearance;
- left shaft wall: `x=26..27`, `y=4..18`;
- open shaft interior: **exactly four cells**, `x=28..31` = **4 u / 64 px**;
- right shaft wall: `x=32..33`, `y=0..16`;
- the right-wall top is a walkable/ledge-climbable surface at `y=17`;
- upper continuation deck: `x=34..45`, `y=16`, with its walkable surface at `y=17`;
- `Exit Staging` is at `(42, 17.08, 0)` on the raised upper-right deck;
- the handler-overhead support remains above the shaft connection, with its main row at `y=20`, leaving a 4 u vertical difference between the raised continuation surface at `y=17` and the support surface at `y=21`.

The four-cell interior width deliberately reuses the human-tested MovementLab wall-kick spacing rather than treating the 24 px Minimum Traversable Gap as a target. The shaft walls remain continuous where Wall Brace is expected, satisfying the authored wall-coverage contract.

The current right-wall production sprite remains the accepted 240 px wall skin for the lower 15 u of the wall. The two newly raised structural rows above it are intentionally left as generic structural cap geometry until a dedicated upper transition/cap is authored; they do not alter the inner `x=32` gameplay plane.

## Intended route

The player crosses the east side of Salvage Intake at floor level, walks through the 4 u-high lower opening, then climbs the 4 u-wide service shaft with Wall Brace / Wall Kick. The lower right wall provides the first reliable brace surface; alternating kicks climb between the two walls. The raised top of the right wall becomes a natural ledge/landing at `y=17`, leading directly onto the east continuation deck.

The raised exit reduces the formerly oversized visual height difference to the crane/handler support while retaining a clear vertical progression through the shaft.

The route is intended to feel like plausible industrial architecture rather than a diagnostic movement station.

## Collision / presentation invariants

The production pipeline keeps the accepted release-critical collision contract:

- hidden `Ground Collision - Hidden` Tilemap remains authoritative;
- Ground layer and existing TilemapCollider2D -> CompositeCollider2D pipeline are unchanged;
- generated collision is refreshed and baked after route refinement;
- the playable shaft interior remains `x=28..31` and is explicitly validated as empty through the raised exit region;
- the lower `x=26..27`, `y=0..3` entrance remains fully open;
- player, Longwatch, native-pixel presentation, penumbra, and movement tuning remain untouched;
- environment dressing sprites do not gain colliders.

The shaft uses production wall sprites over the lower canonical wall planes plus generic structural cap tiles where the raised exit currently extends beyond the existing right-wall sprite. Any later cap/transition art must remain presentation-only and must not intrude into the approved 64 px interior.

## Human validation status

The original `y=15` exit geometry was approved after Play-mode testing. The current production refinement raises that landing to `y=17` while preserving the same 4 u / 64 px shaft width and lower entry.

The raised exit must therefore receive a quick human traversal re-test after applying the production setup. Acceptance remains:

- lower entry is comfortable and does not wedge the standing player;
- first right-wall contact supports Wall Brace naturally;
- repeated alternating Wall Kicks remain comfortable at 4 u / 64 px width;
- the player can consistently reach the raised upper-right ledge without pixel-perfect input;
- the raised exit deck reads as continuation of the level rather than the end of a test course.

Any future geometry change to width, entry height, wall heights, or exit level should likewise be treated as a traversal-contract change and re-tested accordingly.
